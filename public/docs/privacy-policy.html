Penny Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026
Official Google Play Store Policy

1. Overview & Our Commitment

Welcome to Penny ("we", "us", "our", or "the Application"). Penny is an expense tracking and group bill-splitting application designed to help users track personal spending, manage shared debts, and settle dues seamlessly. We take your privacy seriously and believe in absolute transparency regarding what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is secured.

Core Promise: We only collect information essential to providing and improving the service. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

2. Information We Collect and Purpose

Penny collects the following specific data items strictly for the stated purposes:

  • Email Address: Collected during registration and login to create a unique identifier for your account, authenticate you securely via One-Time Passcodes (OTP), prevent unauthorized account access, and link your private expense records and group ledgers so they remain synchronized across your devices.
  • Display Name (Optional): Used to identify you in shared groups and bills so your friends know who created an expense or who owes money.
  • Expense & Financial Records: Expense entries (amounts, categories, descriptions, dates, group associations, and split shares) are stored securely in our database. We store this information strictly to deliver expense tracking, calculate balance settlements, maintain system reliability, and support future application updates and features (such as recurring expenses, multi-currency conversions, and historical analytics). We will only use your expense records for this purpose.
  • UPI ID / VPA (Optional): Collected strictly and optionally to facilitate direct peer-to-peer debt settlements via external UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with zero intermediate fees. Penny does NOT collect or store bank account numbers, ATM PINs, UPI MPINs, or debit/credit card details.

3. Advertisements & Free Service Model

To provide most of our features free of charge and maintain ongoing cloud server hosting, database uptime, and active software development, Penny may display non-intrusive advertisements (such as Google AdMob or banner ads).

  • Third-party ad networks may use standard device advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) according to Google Play policies.
  • Third-party advertisers do not have access to your personal expense details, budget numbers, or group balances.
  • In future updates, Penny may offer an optional in-app purchase to remove all ads completely.

4. Direct UPI Settlements & Security

Penny provides 1-tap debt settlement via deep-links to UPI applications installed on your Android device (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe). Here is how settlement works:

  • Penny generates a standard UPI URI (upi://pay?pa=...) containing only the payee's UPI ID, amount, and note.
  • When you tap "Settle", the transfer executes directly inside your bank-authorized UPI app.
  • Penny never acts as an intermediary, never holds user funds, and never processes monetary payments directly.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls to safeguard your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern HTTPS (TLS 1.3). Cleartext traffic is disabled.
  • Password Hashing: Account passwords are protected using salted bcrypt hashing. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Token Security: Authenticated sessions use cryptographically signed JWT tokens with automatic expiration and revokable refresh tokens.

6. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You retain complete control over your personal data at all times, with two ways to delete your account:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Penny → Go to Settings → Tap Delete Account in the Danger Zone. All personal data will be purged immediately.
  • Web-Based Deletion Portal: In full compliance with Google Play Store data safety mandates, you can initiate account and data deletion outside the application at our public portal: https://penny-37mv.onrender.com/delete-account.

Upon deletion, your account, authentication tokens, reminders, and associated private records are permanently wiped from active servers.

7. Children's Privacy

Penny is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates and regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted here and notified via the app.
