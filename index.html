01 / PERSONAL TRACKING

Lightning-Fast Logging With Zero Mental Friction

Adding an expense shouldn't feel like doing taxes. Penny's dedicated custom keypad lets you enter the amount, assign a category (Food, Groceries, Travel, Entertainment), add a note, and save in under four seconds.

  • Integer arithmetic in paise: Eliminates floating-point rounding discrepancies.
  • Offline-First SQLite caching: Never wait for network bars to record a spend.
  • Instant Mode Switch: Flip between Personal spend and Group bill in 1 tap.
02 / GROUP SPLITTING

Transparent Shared Balances For Flatmates & Trips

Say goodbye to awkward balance arguments and "who paid for what" confusion. Create dedicated group ledgers for apartment rent, vacation expenses, or dinner outings. Every participant sees identical, synchronized balances in real-time.

  • Permanent Reusable Invites: Friends join with a simple invite code or custom deep-link scheme.
  • Flexible Split Logic: Support for equal splits, exact custom rupee amounts, or percentage shares.
  • Push Notifications via FCM: Instant background alerts when a group member logs an expense.
03 / BUDGETS & INSIGHTS

See Where Every Rupee Goes with Interactive Visuals

Take full control of your monthly cashflow. Penny visualizes your spending patterns with clean donut breakdowns, weekly trend comparisons, and category percentages so you spot lifestyle creep before it hurts.

  • Monthly Budget Progress Ring: Live status shows whether you're under or over budget.
  • Category Breakdown: Instant insights into dining, transit, shopping, utilities, and rent.
  • Export Anytime (Free): Download comprehensive CSV, JSON, or PDF statements whenever needed.
04 / 1-TAP UPI SETTLEMENT

Settle Debts Directly via Google Pay & PhonePe

Never copy-paste bank accounts or ask for UPI IDs again. When it's time to settle, Penny prepares the exact transfer URI. Tapping "Pay Now" seamlessly launches your preferred UPI app on your phone with the payee's VPA and amount pre-filled.

  • Peer-to-Peer Direct: No intermediate wallet or Penny wallet balance. Transactions happen directly bank-to-bank.
  • WhatsApp Shareable Slips: Generate clean text summary receipts to post into group chats in 1 tap.
  • Audit Logged: Settle records are stored permanently in the Supabase PostgreSQL ledger.

VERIFIABLE PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

Your Financial Life Belongs to You. Period.

We don't harvest your SMS inbox, we don't sell data to financial brokers, and we never hold your funds.

HTTPS & TLS 1.3 Encryption

All communications between the Flutter client and the NestJS backend are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 with cleartext traffic explicitly disabled.

Hardware-Backed Android Keystore

Sensitive authentication tokens and local credentials are encrypted using hardware cryptography via FlutterSecureStorage on device.

Zero SMS or Contact Scraping

Unlike traditional expense trackers that read every personal SMS message and upload your phonebook, Penny asks for neither. We have zero interest in your personal messages.

Peer-to-Peer UPI (Zero Intermediary)

Penny is not a payment processor and never holds user balances. Debts are settled directly peer-to-peer via official UPI deep-links inside Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm.

Supabase PostgreSQL ACID Ledger & Instant Account Erasure

All transactions and splits are stored with strict ACID relational consistency. Want to leave? You can permanently delete your entire account and all transaction records instantly—either directly inside app settings or via our public Data Safety web portal at /docs/delete-account.html.
